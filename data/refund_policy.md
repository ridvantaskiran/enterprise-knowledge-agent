
# Refund Policy

## Standard Refund Window
Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days of delivery.

## Exceptions
Requests submitted after 30 days require manual review by the Customer Support Manager. A late request is not automatically approved.

## Damaged or Defective Items
Customers should provide the order number and a description of the damage. Supporting photographs may be requested.

## Refund Processing
Once approved, refunds are returned to the original payment method. Processing typically takes 5–10 business days.

## Approval Authority
The Customer Support Manager makes the final decision on exception requests.
